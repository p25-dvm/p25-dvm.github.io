Motorola CDM1250 Site

This section describes the process of building a P25 repeater using the DVMProject’s DVM-V1 Duplex Modem, a Raspberry Pi, and two Motorola CDM1250 radios (any CDM Series radio will work). While this guide outlines one approach, there are many different ways to set up a portable DIY repeater, and this is only one example.

  • Note: that this build is intended solely for hobbyist use. It should never be relied upon for for use in business, production environments or in emergency situations of any context where lives may be at risk or dependant upon reliable communication. The purpose here is to explore, learn, and expand knowledge of digital radio systems, not to create a mission-critical communications solution.

Overview

  • The repeater that this guide will cover configuring has the following parameters:
    • Compute: Raspberry Pi (Configured in the same way from the Conventional Hotspot setup guide).
    • Modem: DVM-V1 Duplex Modem (
    • Name: RPTR-1 (replace with your desired name, such as "John Smith's Repeater" or "Alpha-RPTR-1")
  • By the end of the repeater configuration you will be able transmit from your radio to the repeater at Site A and receive the transmission on a radio witihin range at Site B. Environmental factors as well as antenna quality and position will determine the effective range.
